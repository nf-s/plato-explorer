Nodes and connections

Larger nodes have more incoming mentions. Thicker lines have more mentions between the two articles. Dashed lines are related-entry links without visible text mentions. Counts and sizes reflect the common-term filter.

In the connection list, “out” counts mentions in the selected article; “in” counts mentions of it in the other article.

Common-term filter

Enabled by default. It hides inferred mentions of article titles appearing in more than half the corpus. This reduces generic connections to terms such as Time, Object, and Life. Explicit related-entry links are preserved. Turn it off in Filters to restore every mention.

People and colours

People, Topics, and Keywords can be shown independently. The selected node stays visible regardless of these filters. People without a dedicated article and extracted keywords link to passages in the articles that mention them. Subject colours are approximate labels inferred from titles, introductions, and related entries; they are not SEP categories. Node type and connection count are alternative colour modes.

Mentions

Select an article, then Shift-click a connected node or use its Mentions button. Passages show the actual matches from each available source article.

Full-text search

Search matches all entered words anywhere in an article, ignoring case and accents. Text results cover every article regardless of graph filters. Select a result to read its matching passage.

Data

Matching uses whole-word article titles and curated name aliases. It ignores case and excludes self-mentions, headings, contents lists, and bibliographies. A match can be ambiguous; it does not imply agreement or a citation.

The reader retains all detected links, including common terms. Article text is a local snapshot and may differ from the current encyclopedia.
